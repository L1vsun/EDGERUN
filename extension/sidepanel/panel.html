You are set up. Three things to try.
  1. Open X and scroll. Any post naming a contract gets a strip under it. Nothing to configure, and posts that only name a ticker are left alone on purpose.
  2. Open someone's profile. A card there shows what they have put in front of you and how it turned out. It starts empty - hit read their recent posts and it fills. This is the half no scanner has, because it only exists in your browser.
  3. Check an accusation. The claims tab runs somebody else's evidence against the chain, so you never have to take their word for it.

Or paste any contract address in the box above. Nothing you do here leaves this machine.

Paste an accusation someone sent you. The evidence in it runs here, against the endpoints it names, so you never have to take the reporter's word for it. You get reproduced, contradicted, or an honest "could not check".

Claims come from the copy claim button, which appears on a token whose finding can be written as runnable evidence - an impersonation, a blocked transfer, a name borrowed from another chain. Not every flagged token has one: a verdict that rests on prose cannot be made into a claim, which is the point of the format.
